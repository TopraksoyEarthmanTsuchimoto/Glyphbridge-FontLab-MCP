## 14. TO DO list (v11: the K-series of kerning live tests comes FIRST; the v10 T-series follows)

**Principles (unchanged since v8).** (1) Reading real glyphs correctly and writing geometry that a designer needs come before more testing of what already works. (2) Every item starts with a names-only probe; anything that writes has a gated test on a fresh disposable glyph; every new TOOL is PROPOSED first and built only after "Yes. Build vX as described." (3) One variable per test; predict the numbers first; FontLab's own numbers (GUI readouts, margins) are the oracle. (4) Everything involving glyphs of more than about 150 nodes is in the LAST block by the user's decision. (5) (v10) the user may restart FontLab without saving at any time: tests are re-runnable and state their stage.

**CLOSED (do not repeat):** v8 list (sidebearings and width on a curved glyph; O with counter vs the GUI; diagonal-node circle; S-like blob; smooth gate at 0.33 / 2.73 degrees, zero-length handle on a smooth node, c1 = c2, line-to-handle smooth node, negative x; refusals create no layer; 180- and 990-node draws; `fontlab_status` version reporting). v10: the stale legacy view (fixed at the source by `Update(flGlyph)` and by the 0.88 fallback); the guard live (139a / 139b, 142: a drawing survives a create, and a synced glyph survives a later `font.update()`); first glyph of a new font (layer `Regular` from the package master); margin comparison on a scaled and on a skewed glyph (143); `fillFgData` is useless; sidebearings and width are carried by the sync (141, 142).
